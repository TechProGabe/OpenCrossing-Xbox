The first full release of OpenCrossing-Xbox: Animal Crossing (GameCube, USA) running natively on an original Xbox.

Download `OpenCrossing-Xbox-v1.zip` below and follow the install steps in the [README](https://github.com/TechProGabe/OpenCrossing-Xbox#download). You need your own Animal Crossing (USA, GAFE01) disc image. Coming from a beta? Copy the new `default.xbe` over the old one. Your saves and settings carry over.

## Steadier and faster

This release is mostly about the game holding up for long sessions and keeping its frame rate.

- **Fewer crashes and freezes.** The GPU is waited on properly, so it can't stall the console, the sound chip starts and stops cleanly, a bad pointer in a model is skipped instead of crashing, and Clu Clu Land D no longer takes the game down. The watchdog that catches hangs can't get stuck itself any more. When something does go wrong, the game writes a crash or hang report and keeps the previous run's logs too.
- **Smoother frame rate.** Town holds 58 to 60 fps at 720p, frames are paced to the TV's refresh instead of a timer, and the game no longer waits on the GPU at the end of every frame. Busy spots got cheaper too: a texture lookup that ate almost a tenth of a frame is now nearly free.
- **NES games at full speed.** They run at close to 60 fps (up from about 50), in proper 4:3 with side bars, and at 720p.
- **Sound that keeps up.** The audio no longer stutters when the game is busy.
- **Memory to spare.** Textures take far less room, and 720p no longer runs out of texture space in busy scenes.
- **Faster loading.** The disc image is read in big chunks instead of 1 KB at a time, which also fixes the stutter in the first title demo after a cold boot.

## New

- **Automatic video setup.** The game picks the best mode your dashboard allows (720p, 480p or 480i) and follows its widescreen setting. You can still pin a mode in Options. If your TV goes black, hold Back while the game starts for 480i.
- **A redesigned Options menu**, drawn in the game's own style, with help for each setting.
- **Screenshots** (click the right stick) and an **FPS counter**, both off by default, in Options > Video.
- **Saves under any name** load, so a Dolphin export like `01-GAFE-DobutsunomoriP_MURA.gci` works as it is.
- **Visiting a friend's town** with their save in `save\card_b`. This one is new on the Xbox, so back up both saves first.
- **40% stick dead zones by default**, so older controllers with loose sticks don't walk on their own. Lower it in Options > Controls if yours is in good shape.

## Fixed

- The in-game clock no longer starts in 2083 or jumps hours after a reboot. It follows your dashboard's time zone.
- The player's shirt and the glove in the pockets menu no longer flicker.
- Restart from Options brings you back into the game instead of the dashboard.
- With no other town to visit, the train no longer drops you on the title screen.
- "Clear village data" and erasing a player now actually stick instead of coming back on the next load.
- 128 MB consoles and consoles with an upgraded CPU are handled properly.

## Known issues

- Clu Clu Land D is a Famicom Disk System game and doesn't run yet. Picking it sends you back to the room.
- At 16:9 some 2D art, like the title logo, is a little too wide, and the title menu text sits behind the player and the trees.
- No sound after a crash? Power the console fully off and on.

The full list is in [docs/known-issues.md](https://github.com/TechProGabe/OpenCrossing-Xbox/blob/main/docs/known-issues.md). `ac_xbox.map` is only for reading crash logs.
