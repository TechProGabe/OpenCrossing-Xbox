This one started with a crash report and kind of snowballed: a gyroid crash fix, a faster game, and something to do with the Game Boy Advance and e-Reader parts of the game.

Coming from v1 or v1.1? Copy the new `default.xbe` and `default.tbn` over the old ones. Your saves and settings carry over. New here? See the [README](https://github.com/TechProGabe/OpenCrossing-Xbox#download). You need your own Animal Crossing (USA, GAFE01) disc image.

## Runs better

- **Built with LTO and PGO.** Tighter code tuned on real play sessions, and about half a megabyte more free memory.
- **Menus open fast.** The near half-second freeze when opening a menu at 720p is down to under a tenth of a second.

## Disc validation and a new boot screen

The first boot checks every file on your disc image against a known good copy while stamps land on a letter from TechProGabe. It takes about a second and only happens once. Wrong releases, modded images and damaged files are caught up front instead of crashing later. Hold Y and both triggers at boot to check again.

After that you get a quick "Welcome back to town!" screen.

## No GBA? No problem

An Xbox can't connect a Game Boy Advance, an e-Reader or a GameCube memory card, so those parts of the game got reworked. No spoilers. Try Kapp'n, the post office's e-Reader machine and the Porter. Tom Nook also finishes his store without a visitor now, and the GBA options are gone from the menus. If someone gives you something, save to keep it.

## Fixed

- Closing the gyroid storage menu no longer crashes. Same fix for the catalog.
- The address book no longer freezes when flipping pages while writing a letter ([#4](https://github.com/TechProGabe/OpenCrossing-Xbox/issues/4)).

Plus a new logo and README.

## Known issues

- No sound after a crash? Power the console fully off and on.
